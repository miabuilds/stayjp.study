「日本再留計劃」這個名字,講的是一件事:讓想留在日本——念書、工作、生活的人,能把日文準備好。而準備日文最不該有的門檻,就是「錢」。The name "StayJP" (日本再留計劃) is about one thing: helping people who want to study, work, or build a life in Japan get their Japanese ready. And the last thing that should stand between someone and that goal is money.

「學習資源不該因為付不起而被擋在門外。」"Learning shouldn't be locked behind a price you can't pay."

所以 StayJP 的核心——瀏覽全部單字與文法、每天用各個練習工具、每級一套模考——免費、無需信用卡。不塞廣告、不賣資料。你只要專心學。So the core of StayJP — browsing all vocabulary and grammar, using each practice tool daily, one mock exam per level — is free, with no credit card required. No ads, no selling your data. Just study.

那我們怎麼活下去?So how do we keep going?

靠願意支持的人升級 Premium。Premium 解鎖無限次練習、無限模考與進階功能——買的是「更方便」,不是「才能學」。這些支持,讓我們能持續把核心免費開放給每一個還在路上的人。Through people who choose to upgrade to Premium. Premium unlocks unlimited practice, unlimited mock exams, and advanced features — you're paying for convenience, not for permission to learn. That support is what lets us keep the core free for everyone still on the journey.
