About “how long will a small studio last?”

這是最多人問、也最該講清楚的事。以下都寫在服務條款裡,不是口頭承諾。
The most common question — and all of it is in the terms, not just talk.
買斷是「永久」嗎?Is “lifetime” really forever?
誠實說:不是。買斷是「買到服務終止日為止」的使用權。StayJP 是個人工作室,我不能保證無限期營運,所以我把這件事寫進條款,而不是等你買了才發現。 Honestly: no. It is a licence that runs until the service ends. StayJP is a one-person studio and cannot promise to run forever, so this is written into the terms rather than discovered after purchase.
那買斷划算嗎?Is the lifetime plan worth it?
買斷定價大約等於兩年年費。也就是說用滿兩年就回本,之後都是多的。如果你不確定自己會用多久,先訂月費或年費完全合理,之後想換買斷也可以。 It costs roughly two years of the annual plan. Break even at two years; everything after that is upside. If you are unsure how long you will use it, starting monthly or annually is perfectly reasonable.
如果哪天真的收攤呢?What if the service does shut down?
條款寫明:會至少提前 30 天公告並寄信通知,讓你有時間把想學的學完、把資料匯出帶走。如果服務轉手給別人經營,買斷權益會一起移轉繼續有效。 The terms require at least 30 days' notice by announcement and email, so you can finish what you are studying and export your data. If the service is transferred to another operator, lifetime access transfers with it.
內容還會繼續加嗎?Will content keep growing?
會,而且這頁就是給你檢查用的。近兩個月有 44 天推出更新;上面的時間軸會持續往下長。我不會給「每週幾篇」這種硬承諾,但更新節奏你可以自己看。 Yes — and this page exists so you can check. We shipped on 44 days over the past two months, and the timeline above keeps growing. No fixed weekly quota is promised; the pace is simply visible.
為什麼相信你會一直做?Why keep building it?
因為我自己就是使用者。我人在日本、也在準備日檢,StayJP 是我每天在用的工具——功能都是我自己卡關才做出來的。我停止更新的那天,大概也是我自己不需要它的那天。 Because I use it myself. I live in Japan and study for the JLPT; StayJP is the tool I open every day, and most features exist because I hit that wall first.
我的學習紀錄帶得走嗎?Can I take my data with me?
可以。學習紀錄、生詞本、錯題本都能匯出。帳號資料也可以隨時要求刪除。 Yes. Study history, your word notebook and wrong-answer notes can all be exported, and account data can be deleted on request.
