StayJP(日本再留計劃)是一個免費的日文學習網站與 App。從五十音之後到 N1,單字、文法、模考、複習一站搞定——大部分功能免費就能用。StayJP is a free Japanese-learning website and app. From beyond kana all the way to N1 — vocabulary, grammar, mock exams, and spaced review in one place. Most of it is free to use.

為什麼免費Why it's free

「日本再留計劃」這個名字講的是一件事:讓想留在日本——念書、工作、生活的人,能把日文準備好。而準備日文最不該有的門檻,就是「錢」。The name "StayJP" is about one thing: helping people who want to stay in Japan — to study, work, or live — get their Japanese ready. And the last thing that should stand in the way is money.

所以 StayJP 的核心——瀏覽全部單字與文法、每天用各個練習工具、每級一套模考——免費、無需信用卡。不塞廣告、不賣資料,你只要專心學。So StayJP's core — browsing all vocabulary and grammar, using each practice tool daily, one mock exam per level — is free, no credit card. No ads, no selling your data. Just focus on learning.

那我們怎麼活下去?靠願意支持的人升級 Premium——解鎖無限次練習與進階功能。買的是「更方便」,不是「才能學」。這些支持,讓我們能持續把核心免費開放給每一個還在路上的人,並由獨立小團隊持續更新內容。So how do we keep going? Through people who choose Premium — unlocking unlimited practice and advanced features. You're paying for convenience, not for the right to learn. That support lets us keep the core free for everyone still on the way, maintained by a small independent team that keeps adding content.
