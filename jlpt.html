JLPT 怎麼考、怎麼計分?How the JLPT works and is scored

考試日期與報名 Dates & registration

一年舉辦兩次,通常在 7 月與 12 月的第一個星期日(部分海外考區一年只有一次)。報名多在考前約 2–3 個月開放,名額有限、常會額滿,建議提早卡位。Held twice a year, usually the first Sunday of July and December (some overseas sites run once a year). Registration opens ~2–3 months ahead and can fill up — sign up early.

考試內容 Sections

分三大塊:言語知識(文字・語彙・文法)、読解(閱讀)、聴解(聽力)。N4、N5 把「言語知識+読解」合併計分;N1–N3 三塊分開計分。全程約 2–3 小時,依級別而定。Three areas: Language Knowledge (vocab/grammar), Reading, and Listening. N4/N5 combine Language Knowledge + Reading into one score; N1–N3 score all three separately. The test runs ~2–3 hours depending on level.

計分與合格 Scoring & passing

總分 0–180。除了要達到整體合格分(N5:80、N4:90、N3:95、N2:90、N1:100),每一節也要過最低門檻(各節約 19/60)。就算總分夠,只要有一節掛零式偏低也不合格——所以三科要平均顧,不能只拚單字。Total 0–180. You must clear both the overall pass mark (N5:80, N4:90, N3:95, N2:90, N1:100) and a per-section minimum (~19/60 each). A high total won't save you if one section falls below its floor — so balance all three, don't only grind vocab.

常見問題FAQ

JLPT 一年考幾次?How often is the JLPT held?

一年兩次,通常在 7 月與 12 月;部分海外考區一年只有一次。報名約在考前 2–3 個月開放。Twice a year, usually July and December; some overseas sites run once a year. Registration opens ~2–3 months before.

在日本工作要 N 幾?What level do I need to work in Japan?

沒有統一標準,但多數企業求職以 N2 為常見門檻,專業職常要 N1。高度人材簽證:N2 加 10 分、N1 加 15 分。There's no single rule, but N2 is a common hiring bar and professional roles often want N1. HSP visa: +10 for N2, +15 for N1.

從零到 N2 要多久?How long from zero to N2?

因人而異,常見估計是 600 小時以上的有效學習。華語母語者因漢字優勢,閱讀通常較快,但聽力與文法仍需扎實累積。It varies; a common estimate is 600+ hours of effective study. Chinese speakers often read faster thanks to kanji, but listening and grammar still take steady work.

一定要按 N5→N1 順序考嗎?Do I have to take them in order?

不用。可以直接報你有把握的級別,很多人第一次就考 N3 或 N2。No. Register for whatever level you can handle — many people start at N3 or N2.

總分夠了卻沒過,為什麼?I hit the total but still failed — why?

因為每一節都有最低分門檻(各節約 19/60)。只要有一節低於門檻,即使總分達標也不合格。三科要平均準備。Each section has a minimum (~19/60). Fall below it in any one section and you fail even with a passing total. Balance all three.
