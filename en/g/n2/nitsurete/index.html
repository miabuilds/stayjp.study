～につれて

JLPT N2・Other

Meaning

"as ~ / in proportion as ~." Attaches to the dictionary form or a noun. Indicates that two things change together, gradually and in tandem.

Pattern

dictionary form ・ noun ＋ につれて

Examples

  • 時間が経つにつれて、痛みが和らいだ。 As time passed, the pain eased.
  • 季節が進むにつれて、朝晩の寒暖差が大きくなってきた。 As the season progressed, the temperature gap between morning and night grew larger.
  • AI の進化につれて、働き方も変わってきた。 As AI has evolved, the way we work has changed too.
