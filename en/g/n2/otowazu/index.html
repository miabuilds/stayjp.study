～を問わず

JLPT N2・Scope / Limitation

Meaning

"Regardless of ~ / irrespective of ~." Indicates that something is not restricted by the stated condition. Attaches to a noun (often a noun expressing a range of possibilities, such as age, gender, or presence/absence).

Pattern

noun ＋ を問わず

Examples

  • 年齢を問わず、どなたでもご参加いただけます。 Regardless of age, anyone is welcome to take part.
  • 経験の有無を問わず、応募を歓迎します。 Regardless of whether you have experience or not, we welcome your application.
  • 国籍を問わず、このイベントへの参加が可能だ。 Regardless of nationality, anyone can participate in this event.
