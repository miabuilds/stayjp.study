～がたい

JLPT N2・Other

Meaning

"hard to ~ / difficult to bring oneself to ~." Expresses that something is psychologically/emotionally difficult to do (not physically impossible). Attaches to the verb stem (masu-form minus ます). Literary/written style.

Pattern

masu-form (drop ます) ＋ がたい

Examples

  • 彼のあの行動は今でも理解しがたい。 Even now, I find that action of his hard to understand.
  • 信じがたいほど幸運な出来事だった。 It was an almost unbelievably lucky event.
  • この結果は受け入れがたいが、現実として認めるしかない。 This result is hard to accept, but I have no choice but to acknowledge it as reality.
