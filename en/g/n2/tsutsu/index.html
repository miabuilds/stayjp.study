～つつ（も）

JLPT N2・Contrast / Concession

Meaning

"While ~ / even though ~." A written expression indicating that one does (or fails to do) something even while fully aware of the contrary. Attaches to the ます-stem of a verb.

Pattern

masu-form (stem) ＋ つつ（も）

Examples

  • 体に悪いと知りつつも、夜更かしがやめられない。 Even though I know it's bad for my health, I can't stop staying up late.
  • 申し訳ないと思いつつ、返事を後回しにしてしまった。 Even while feeling apologetic, I ended up putting off replying.
  • 急ぎだと言われつつも、つい他の仕事を優先してしまった。 Even though I'd been told it was urgent, I ended up prioritizing other work anyway.
