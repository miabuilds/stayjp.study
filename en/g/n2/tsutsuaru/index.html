～つつある

JLPT N2・Time / Change

Meaning

"To be gradually ~ing." Expresses an ongoing process of change moving steadily in one direction. Written/formal register. Attaches to the ます-stem of a verb.

Pattern

masu-form (stem) ＋ つつある

Examples

  • 地球の平均気温は上昇しつつある。 The Earth's average temperature is gradually rising.
  • 伝統工芸の技術が失われつつある。 Traditional craft techniques are gradually being lost.
  • 都市部の空き家問題が深刻化しつつある。 The vacant-house problem in urban areas is gradually worsening.
