～かねる

JLPT N2・Sentence-ending Expressions

Meaning

"Find it hard to ~ / be unable to ~." A polite way to decline or to express difficulty in doing something.

Pattern

masu-form (drop ます) ＋ かねる

Examples

  • そのご要望にはお答えしかねます。 I'm afraid I'm unable to answer that request.
  • その結論には賛成しかねる。 I find it hard to agree with that conclusion.
  • 大変申し訳ございませんが、当日の配送はお引き受けしかねます。 We are very sorry, but we are unable to accept same-day delivery.
