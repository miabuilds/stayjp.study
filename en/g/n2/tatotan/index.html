～たとたん（に）

JLPT N2・Time / Change

Meaning

"The moment ~ / as soon as ~." The second event happens immediately, right after the first action is completed. Attaches to the た-form of a verb. Often the second event is unexpected or beyond one's control.

Pattern

ta-form ＋ とたん（に）

Examples

  • 玄関のドアを開けたとたん、愛犬が飛びついてきた。 The moment I opened the front door, my dog came leaping at me.
  • 薬を飲んだとたんに、猛烈な眠気に襲われた。 The moment I took the medicine, I was hit by overwhelming drowsiness.
  • 駅を出たとたん、激しい夕立に遭った。 The moment I stepped out of the station, I got caught in a violent evening downpour.
