～ばかりか・ばかりでなく

JLPT N2・Degree / Emphasis

Meaning

"Not only ~ but also ~." Adds a further, often more striking, item on top of the first.

Pattern

plain form ＋ ばかりか

Examples

  • 日本語ばかりか、中国語も流暢に話せる。 Not only Japanese, but he can also speak Chinese fluently.
  • 遅刻したばかりか、資料も忘れてきた。 Not only was he late, but he also forgot to bring the materials.
  • 彼は仕事ができるばかりか、人望も厚い。 Not only is he good at his job, but he is also well respected.
