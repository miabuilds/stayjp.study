～ながら（も）

JLPT N2・Contrast / Concession

Meaning

"Although ~ / while ~." Expresses a contrast between two facts. Also common in speech. Attaches to the ます-stem of a verb, or to a noun or adjective.

Pattern

masu-form (stem) ＋ ながら（も）／noun・adjective ＋ ながら（も）

Examples

  • 残念ながら、今回は選考に漏れました。 Regrettably, you were not selected this time.
  • 小さいながらも、自分の事務所を構えている。 Small though it is, he has set up his own office.
  • 狭いながらも、住み心地のいい部屋だ。 Cramped though it is, it's a room that's comfortable to live in.
