～にしても

JLPT N2・Contrast / Concession

Meaning

"Even if ~ / even granting that ~." Concedes a point for the sake of argument, yet the conclusion remains unchanged. Attaches to the plain form. When repeated (「A にしても B にしても」) it means "whether A or B."

Pattern

plain form ＋ にしても

Examples

  • 忙しかったにしても、一言連絡ぐらいはできるはずだ。 Even if you were busy, you could at least have dropped a quick message.
  • 冗談だったにしても、言っていい内容ではない。 Even if it was a joke, it's not the sort of thing you should say.
  • 電車にしてもバスにしても、朝の時間帯は混む。 Whether by train or by bus, the morning hours are crowded.
