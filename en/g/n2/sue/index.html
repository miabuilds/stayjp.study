～末（に）

JLPT N2・Time / Change

Meaning

"After ~ / as the result of ~ (finally)." Indicates that a result is reached after much effort, struggle, or a long process. Used as 「Noun の末（に）」 or 「た-form ＋末（に）」.

Pattern

noun ＋ の ＋ 末（に）／ta-form ＋ 末（に）

Examples

  • 数時間の議論の末に、ようやく結論が出た。 After several hours of discussion, a conclusion was finally reached.
  • 散々悩んだ末、留学を決意した。 After agonizing over it endlessly, I made up my mind to study abroad.
  • 家族で話し合った末に、郊外への引っ越しを決めた。 After talking it over as a family, we decided to move out to the suburbs.
