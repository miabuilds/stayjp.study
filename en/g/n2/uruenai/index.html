～得る・得ない

JLPT N2・Sentence-ending Expressions

Meaning

"Can ~ / is possible ~ (得る) / cannot ~ / is impossible (得ない)." Expresses possibility. The affirmative form reads either "uru" or "eru"; the negative is always "enai."

Pattern

masu-form (drop ます) ＋ 得る（うる／える）

Examples

  • そんなことは現実的にあり得ない。 Realistically, such a thing is impossible.
  • 誰にでも起こり得る身近な問題だ。 It's a familiar problem that can happen to anyone.
  • 地震はいつでも起こり得るので、防災意識を持とう。 An earthquake can strike at any time, so let's stay disaster-conscious.
