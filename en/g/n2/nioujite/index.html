～に応じて

JLPT N2・Scope / Limitation

Meaning

"According to ~ / in accordance with ~." Indicates that something changes or is adjusted in response to varying circumstances (each case differs depending on the noun).

Pattern

noun ＋ に応じて

Examples

  • 成果に応じて、ボーナスが決まる。 The bonus is determined according to your results.
  • 状況に応じて、柔軟に対応する。 We respond flexibly according to the situation.
  • 季節に応じて、メニューを入れ替えている。 We change the menu according to the season.
