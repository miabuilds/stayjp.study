～上に

JLPT N2・Topic / Evaluation

Meaning

"Not only ~ but also ~ / on top of ~." Adds a further quality on top of one that already exists (both usually of the same positive or negative tone).

Pattern

plain form ＋ 上に

Examples

  • この店は価格が手頃な上に、料理の質も高い。 This restaurant is reasonably priced, and on top of that the food quality is high.
  • 彼は頭脳明晰な上に、運動神経も抜群だ。 He has a sharp mind, and on top of that his athletic ability is outstanding.
  • このカフェは駅に近い上に、Wi-Fi も高速だ。 This cafe is close to the station, and on top of that the Wi-Fi is fast.
