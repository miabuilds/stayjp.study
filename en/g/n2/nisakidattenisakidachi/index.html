～に先立って・に先立ち

JLPT N2・Time / Change

Meaning

"prior to ~ / before ~." Attaches to a noun. Used in formal settings to indicate preparation or notice given before an event takes place.

Pattern

noun ＋ に先立って／に先立ち

Examples

  • 開会に先立って、主催者が挨拶を述べた。 Prior to the opening, the organizer gave a greeting.
  • 工事に先立ち、地域住民への説明会が実施された。 Prior to the construction, an information session for local residents was held.
  • 記者会見に先立って、関係者だけで事前打ち合わせを行った。 Prior to the press conference, only the parties involved held a preliminary meeting.
