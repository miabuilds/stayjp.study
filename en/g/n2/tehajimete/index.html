～てはじめて

JLPT N2・Time / Change

Meaning

"only after doing ~ (did one finally ...)." Attaches to the te-form. Emphasizes that one realized or understood something only after experiencing it.

Pattern

te-form ＋ はじめて

Examples

  • 入院してはじめて、健康のありがたさを痛感した。 It was only after being hospitalized that I keenly felt how precious health is.
  • 自分で料理を作ってはじめて、母の苦労がわかった。 Only after cooking for myself did I understand how hard my mother had worked.
  • 現地で暮らしてはじめて、その街の魅力が理解できた。 It was only after living there that I could understand the charm of that town.
