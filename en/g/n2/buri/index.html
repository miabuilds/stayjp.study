～ぶり

JLPT N2・Other

Meaning

"in the manner of ~" / "after an interval of ~." Attaches to a noun. (1) With action/manner nouns it describes the way something is done. (2) With a period of time it means "for the first time in ~ / after ~ have passed."

Pattern

noun ＋ ぶり

Examples

  • 彼の話しぶりから、自信を感じた。 From the way he spoke, I sensed his confidence.
  • 5年ぶりに母校を訪れた。 I visited my alma mater for the first time in 5 years.
  • 10年ぶりに高校のクラスメイトと再会した。 I was reunited with my high school classmates for the first time in 10 years.
