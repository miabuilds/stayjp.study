～ものの

JLPT N2・Contrast / Concession

Meaning

"Although ~, but ~." Acknowledges the fact in the first clause, but the result falls short of, or runs counter to, what one would expect. Attaches to the plain form.

Pattern

plain form ＋ ものの

Examples

  • チケットを買ったものの、結局行けなかった。 Although I bought a ticket, in the end I couldn't go.
  • 説明は受けたものの、操作方法がよくわからない。 Although I received an explanation, I still don't really understand how to operate it.
  • ジムに入会したものの、忙しくて一度しか行っていない。 Although I joined the gym, I've been so busy I've only gone once.
