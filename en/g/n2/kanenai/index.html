～かねない

JLPT N2・Sentence-ending Expressions

Meaning

"Might well ~ / could end up ~ (something bad)." Indicates that an undesirable outcome is possible.

Pattern

masu-form (drop ます) ＋ かねない

Examples

  • このまま対策を怠れば、大事故に発展しかねない。 If we keep neglecting countermeasures, this could well develop into a major accident.
  • 無理を続けると、体を壊しかねない。 If you keep overdoing it, you might end up ruining your health.
  • 睡眠不足が続くと、判断ミスを招きかねない。 Continued lack of sleep could lead to errors in judgment.
