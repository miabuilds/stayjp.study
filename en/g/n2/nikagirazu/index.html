～に限らず

JLPT N2・Scope / Limitation

Meaning

"Not limited to ~ / not only ~." Indicates that the scope extends beyond just the stated item to a wider range. Attaches to a noun.

Pattern

noun ＋ に限らず

Examples

  • 若者に限らず、高齢者にもスマホは浸透している。 Not just among young people, smartphones have spread to the elderly as well.
  • 日本に限らず、アジア全体で少子化が進んでいる。 Not only in Japan, but across all of Asia the birthrate is declining.
  • 週末に限らず、平日もリモートワーク可能な会社が増えた。 Not just on weekends, the number of companies allowing remote work on weekdays too has increased.
