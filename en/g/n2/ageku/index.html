～あげく（に）

JLPT N2・Time / Change

Meaning

"after (a long process of) ~, in the end ..." Attaches to the ta-form or to a noun with の. Describes a result reached after much effort or trouble, usually an unfavorable or disappointing outcome.

Pattern

ta-form ＋ あげく（に）／noun の ＋ あげく（に）

Examples

  • さんざん迷ったあげく、結局購入を見送った。 After agonizing over it endlessly, I ended up deciding not to buy it after all.
  • 長時間の議論のあげく、結論は出なかった。 After a long discussion, in the end no conclusion was reached.
  • 道に迷ったあげく、結局タクシーで目的地に向かった。 After getting lost, I ended up taking a taxi to my destination after all.
