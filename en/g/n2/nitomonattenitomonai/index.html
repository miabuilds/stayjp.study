～に伴って・に伴い

JLPT N2・Other

Meaning

"along with ~ / as ~." Indicates that as one change occurs, another change accompanies it. Attaches to a noun (名詞＋に伴って) or to a verb via のに伴って.

Pattern

noun ＋ に伴って／dictionary form ＋ のに伴って

Examples

  • 人口の増加に伴って、都市部の住宅不足が深刻化した。 Along with the increase in population, the housing shortage in urban areas grew serious.
  • 経済の発展に伴い、環境負荷も増大した。 As the economy developed, the burden on the environment also increased.
  • リモートワークの普及に伴って、地方移住を選ぶ人が増えている。 As remote work has spread, the number of people choosing to move to rural areas is rising.
