～とはいえ

JLPT N2・Contrast / Concession

Meaning

"That said ~ / even though it's said to be ~." Concedes the first clause as true, but points out that it doesn't fully hold or apply. Attaches to the plain form (or a noun).

Pattern

plain form ＋ とはいえ

Examples

  • 春とはいえ、朝晩はまだ冷え込む。 It may be spring, but mornings and evenings are still chilly.
  • 子供とはいえ、言っていい冗談とそうでない冗談はある。 He may be a child, but there are still jokes that are okay to make and jokes that aren't.
  • 慣れているとはいえ、プレゼン前はやはり緊張する。 Used to it though I am, I still get nervous before a presentation.
