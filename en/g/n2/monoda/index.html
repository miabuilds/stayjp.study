～ものだ (exclamation)

JLPT N2・Sentence-ending Expressions

Meaning

"How ~ it is! / ~ indeed." Used to express admiration, emotion, or heartfelt reflection about a general truth.

Pattern

plain form ＋ ものだ

Examples

  • 時の流れは本当に早いものだ。 How quickly time really does pass!
  • 子供の頃はよくこの川で遊んだものだ。 I used to play in this river a lot when I was a child.
  • 人の一生とは儚いものだ。 A human life is such a fleeting thing.
