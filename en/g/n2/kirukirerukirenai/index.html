～きる・～きれる・～きれない

JLPT N2・Other

Meaning

"do ~ completely / manage to finish ~ (or not)." Attaches to the verb stem (masu-form minus ます). Indicates thorough completion of an action; 「きれる」 means one can finish it, and 「きれない」 means one cannot finish it.

Pattern

masu-form (drop ます) ＋ きる／きれる／きれない

Examples

  • フルマラソンを走りきった。 I ran the full marathon all the way to the end.
  • 一人では食べきれない量が出てきた。 An amount came out that was more than one person could finish eating.
  • 42 キロを走りきった時、自然と涙が出た。 When I finished running the whole 42 kilometers, tears came to my eyes naturally.
