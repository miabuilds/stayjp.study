～にかけて

JLPT N2・Time / Change

Meaning

"From ~ to ~ (over a span or range)." Indicates an approximate span or range from one point to another, in time or space. Used as 「Noun から Noun にかけて」.

Pattern

noun ＋ から ＋ noun ＋ にかけて

Examples

  • 3月から4月にかけて、全国で桜が咲く。 From around March to April, cherry blossoms bloom all across the country.
  • 関東から東北にかけて、明日は荒れ模様の天気となる見込みだ。 From the Kanto region up through Tohoku, tomorrow's weather is expected to turn stormy.
  • 夕方から夜にかけて、全国的に気温が下がる見込みだ。 From evening into the night, temperatures are expected to drop nationwide.
