～っこない

JLPT N2・Sentence-ending Expressions

Meaning

"There's no way ~ / absolutely couldn't ~." A colloquial expression with a strong tone, flatly denying a possibility.

Pattern

masu-form (drop ます) ＋ っこない

Examples

  • そんな高難度の試験、一発で受かりっこない。 There's no way I'll pass such a high-level exam on the first try.
  • この時期に予約なんて取れっこない。 There's no way you can get a reservation at this time of year.
  • 締切まで1日で全部終わらせっこない。 There's no way I can finish all of it in a single day before the deadline.
