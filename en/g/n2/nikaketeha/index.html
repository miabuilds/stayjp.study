～にかけては

JLPT N2・Other

Meaning

"when it comes to ~ (one excels)." Attaches to a noun. Emphasizes that someone's ability is outstanding in one particular area or field.

Pattern

noun ＋ にかけては

Examples

  • 和食にかけては、彼女の右に出る者はいない。 When it comes to Japanese cuisine, no one can surpass her.
  • 記憶力にかけては、同期の誰にも負けない。 When it comes to memory, I won't lose to anyone in my cohort.
  • スピードに関する議論にかけては、彼が社内一だ。 When it comes to debates about speed, he is the best in the company.
