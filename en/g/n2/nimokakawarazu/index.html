～にもかかわらず

JLPT N2・Contrast / Concession

Meaning

"Despite ~ / in spite of ~." A written, formal concessive expression with a fairly strong tone, indicating that a result runs contrary to what the first clause would lead you to expect. Attaches to the plain form (or a noun).

Pattern

plain form ＋ にもかかわらず

Examples

  • 何度も確認したにもかかわらず、同じミスを繰り返した。 Despite having checked it many times, I made the same mistake again.
  • 雨にもかかわらず、多くの来場者が集まった。 In spite of the rain, a large crowd of visitors gathered.
  • 平日にもかかわらず、人気のベーカリーには行列ができていた。 Despite it being a weekday, there was a line at the popular bakery.
