～かける・かけの

JLPT N2・Other

Meaning

"half-done / in the middle of ~-ing." Attaches to the verb stem (masu-form minus ます). Indicates an action that is partway through or unfinished; use 「かけの」 before a noun.

Pattern

masu-form (drop ます) ＋ かける／かけの

Examples

  • 読みかけの小説が本棚に積まれている。 Half-read novels are piled up on the bookshelf.
  • 何か言いかけて、途中でやめた。 He started to say something, then stopped partway.
  • 食べかけのパンを机に置いたまま、電話に出た。 Leaving my half-eaten bread on the desk, I answered the phone.
