～ばかりだ（変化）

JLPT N2・Time / Change

Meaning

"Keeps ~ing / only gets more and more ~." Emphasizes that a state changes in one single direction with no reversal. Often carries a negative nuance, but not always (e.g. 「上達するばかりだ」 = keeps getting better and better). Attaches to the dictionary form of a verb.

Pattern

dictionary form ＋ ばかりだ

Examples

  • 体力は落ちるばかりだ。 My stamina just keeps declining.
  • 業務の負担が増えるばかりだ。 The workload only keeps increasing.
  • 都心のマンション価格は上がるばかりだ。 Apartment prices in the city center just keep rising.
