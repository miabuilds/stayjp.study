～にわたって

JLPT N2・Scope / Limitation

Meaning

"Over (a span of) ~ / spanning ~." Indicates that something extends over a wide range or a long period of time. Attaches to a noun (usually one expressing a length of time or an extent).

Pattern

noun ＋ にわたって

Examples

  • 3日間にわたって国際会議が行われた。 An international conference was held over the course of three days.
  • 広範囲にわたって停電が発生した。 A power outage occurred over a wide area.
  • 20年にわたって、同じ分野の研究を続けてきた。 For over 20 years, I have continued research in the same field.
