Te-form

JLPT N5・Verb Conjugation

Meaning

The most important conjugation, used for requests (〜てください), linking/sequencing actions, and the progressive/state form (〜ている). For godan verbs (sound changes): く→いて, ぐ→いで, す→して, む/ぶ/ぬ→んで, う/つ/る→って. For ichidan verbs: drop る + て (e.g., 見る→見て). Exception: 行く→行って.

Pattern

godan sound change ＋ て／ichidan: drop る ＋ て

Examples

  • 話す→話して、急ぐ→急いで hanasu → hanashite (speak), isogu → isoide (hurry)
  • 休む→休んで、使う→使って yasumu → yasunde (rest), tsukau → tsukatte (use)
