Adverbial use of adjectives

JLPT N5・Adjectives & Modification

Meaning

Using an adjective adverbially to modify a verb. I-adjectives: い→く. Na-adjectives: replace な with に (e.g., 静かな → 静かに).

Pattern

i-adjective→く ＋ verb／na-adjective→に ＋ verb

Examples

  • 静かに歩きます。 To walk quietly.
  • 上手に踊ります。 To dance skillfully.
