Verb masu-form

JLPT N5・Verb Conjugation

Meaning

The most basic polite form. Godan verbs: shift the final -u sound to the -i sound + ます. Ichidan verbs: drop る + ます. Irregular: する→します, 来る→来ます.

Pattern

godan: u-row→i-row ＋ ます／ichidan: drop る ＋ ます

Examples

  • 飲む→飲みます nomu → nomimasu: drink (godan)
  • 着る→着ます kiru → kimasu: wear (ichidan)
