～ます・～ません

JLPT N5・Basic Sentence Patterns

Meaning

The polite present tense. Used with elders and strangers.

Pattern

verb masu-form ＋ ます／ません

Examples

  • 来週、林さんは京都へ出張します。 Next week, Mr. Hayashi will go to Kyoto on a business trip.
  • 田村教授はお酒を飲みません。 Professor Tamura does not drink alcohol.
