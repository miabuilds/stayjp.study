Adjectives modifying nouns

JLPT N5・Adjectives & Modification

Meaning

An adjective placed before a noun to modify it. I-adjectives attach directly; na-adjectives add な.

Pattern

i-adjective ＋ noun／na-adjective ＋ な ＋ noun

Examples

  • 小さい子犬。 A little puppy.
  • 静かな庭。 A quiet garden.
