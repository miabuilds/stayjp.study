Other particles summary

JLPT N5・Particles

Meaning

A summary of more advanced uses of various particles: を (point of separation/leaving, or passing through), に (frequency/contact/change), で (domain of comparison/scope), は (contrast), か (choice).

Pattern

を (leaving/passing through)・に (frequency/contact/change)・で (unit/scope)・は (contrast)・か (choice)

Examples

  • 高校を卒業します。 I will graduate from high school. (separation / leaving)
  • 1か月に2回映画館へ行きます。 I go to the movie theater twice a month. (frequency)
  • チームでいちばん若い。 I'm the youngest on the team. (domain of comparison)
  • 寿司は好きですが、刺身は苦手です。 I like sushi, but I'm not fond of sashimi. (contrast)
  • コーヒーか紅茶、どちらにしますか。 Coffee or tea, which would you like? (choice)
