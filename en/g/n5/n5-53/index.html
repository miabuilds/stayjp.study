Connective use of te-form

JLPT N5・Te-form Uses

Meaning

The te-form links clauses to show parallel description, sequence of actions, cause, or an accompanying circumstance.

Pattern

te-form connects multiple actions or states

Examples

  • 朝起きて、歯を磨いて、出かけます。 I get up in the morning, brush my teeth, and head out. (sequence)
  • お腹が空いて、何か食べました。 I was hungry, so I ate something. (cause)
