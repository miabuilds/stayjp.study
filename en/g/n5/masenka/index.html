～ませんか

JLPT N5・Basic Sentence Patterns

Meaning

A soft, considerate invitation: "Won't you ~?" / "Would you like to ~?" Unlike 「ましょう」, which assumes the listener will join in, 「ませんか」 asks and leaves them free to decline, so it feels more polite.

Pattern

verb masu-form ＋ ませんか

Examples

  • 今度の日曜日、山登りに行きませんか。 Would you like to go hiking this coming Sunday?
  • コーヒーでも飲みませんか。 How about a coffee or something?
