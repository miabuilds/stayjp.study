Polite form and plain form

JLPT N5・Basic Sentence Patterns

Meaning

Polite form is used in formal situations; plain form is used with friends and family.

Pattern

polite form (です・ます) / plain form (だ・dictionary form, etc.)

Examples

  • 週末は晴れです。→ 週末は晴れだ。 It's sunny on the weekend.
  • 本を読んでいます。→ 本を読んでいる。 I'm reading a book.
