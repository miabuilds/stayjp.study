～極まりない・～極まる

JLPT N1・Degree / Comparison

Meaning

"extremely~", "~ to the utmost degree". Emphasizes that the degree is very high. Written style. Attaches to the stem of a な-adjective. 「極まりない」 and 「極まる」 are near-synonyms (both mean 'utterly~'), used mostly with negative-quality adjectives.

Pattern

na-adjective stem ＋極まりない／極まる

Examples

  • 彼のあの態度は失礼極まりない。 That attitude of his is rude in the extreme.
  • 危険極まりない運転で、重大事故につながった。 The extremely dangerous driving led to a serious accident.
  • ラッシュ時の通勤電車は不快極まりない。 The commuter train at rush hour is unpleasant in the extreme.
