～嫌いがある

JLPT N1・Degree & Comparison

Meaning

"To have a tendency to ~" / "To be prone to ~." Refers to an undesirable inclination. Formal/written language. Formed as 「辞書形＋嫌いがある」 (a noun takes 「の」 before 「嫌いがある」).

Pattern

dictionary form ＋ 嫌いがある

Examples

  • 彼には独断で決める嫌いがある。 He has a tendency to decide things arbitrarily on his own.
  • うちの上司は細かい指示を出しすぎる嫌いがある。 Our boss has a tendency to give too many minute, detailed instructions.
