～にせよ…にせよ

JLPT N1・Concession / Contrast

Meaning

"whether it's~ or~". Lists two cases and states that the conclusion is the same for both. More formal/written than the N2 form 「にしろ」. Attaches to nouns or plain forms.

Pattern

noun／plain form ＋にせよ…にせよ

Examples

  • 賛成にせよ反対にせよ、自分の立場を明確にすべきだ。 Whether you agree or disagree, you should make your position clear.
  • 電車にせよタクシーにせよ、ラッシュ時は時間がかかる。 Whether by train or by taxi, it takes a long time during rush hour.
