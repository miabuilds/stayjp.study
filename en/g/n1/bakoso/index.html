～ばこそ

JLPT N1・Cause / Reason

Meaning

"precisely because~, that is why…". More literary and more emphatic than 「からこそ」. Attaches to the conditional (ば) form. Stresses that the stated reason is the sole and essential cause.

Pattern

ba-form ＋こそ

Examples

  • 君の将来を案じていればこそ、厳しいことを言う。 It is precisely because I worry about your future that I say such harsh things.
  • 健康であればこそ、好きなことに挑戦できる。 It is precisely because you are healthy that you can take on the things you love.
