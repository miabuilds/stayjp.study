～ならまだしも

JLPT N1・Limitation / Emphasis

Meaning

"If it were ~, that would be one thing, but…." The first case would be acceptable/tolerable, but the actual (latter) case goes too far. Attaches to a noun or plain form.

Pattern

noun／plain form ＋ならまだしも

Examples

  • 冗談ならまだしも、本気で言っているのか。 If it were a joke, fine — but are you saying this seriously?
  • 5分の遅刻ならまだしも、1時間遅れるのはありえない。 Being five minutes late is one thing, but being an hour late is unacceptable.
  • 小さなミスならまだしも、取引先に損害を与える規模の失敗は看過できない。 A small mistake would be one thing, but a failure on the scale of harming a client can't be overlooked.
