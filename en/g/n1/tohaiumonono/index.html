～とはいうものの

JLPT N1・Concession / Contrast

Meaning

"having said that, but…", "that may be true, but…". More conversational and concrete than the N2 form 「とはいえ」. Concedes the first point, then presents a contrasting reality. Attaches to the plain form.

Pattern

plain form ＋とはいうものの

Examples

  • 便利になったとはいうものの、失われたものも少なくない。 Things have become more convenient, but there is no small amount that has been lost, too.
  • 円高だとはいうものの、海外旅行は依然として高く感じる。 The yen may be strong, but overseas travel still feels expensive.
