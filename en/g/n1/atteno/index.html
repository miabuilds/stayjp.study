～あっての

JLPT N1・Cause & Reason

Meaning

"There is ... only because there is ~" / "~ is what makes ... possible." Emphasizes that the first item is the essential prerequisite for the existence of the second. Formed as 「名詞＋あっての＋名詞」.

Pattern

noun ＋ あっての

Examples

  • お客様あっての商売だ。 A business exists only because there are customers.
  • 健康あっての挑戦だ。体を壊しては元も子もない。 It's only with your health that you can take on challenges; if you ruin your body, it all comes to nothing.
