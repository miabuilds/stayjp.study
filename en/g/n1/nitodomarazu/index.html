～にとどまらず

JLPT N1・Miscellaneous

Meaning

"Not limited to ~" / "Not stopping at ~." Something extends beyond a certain scope and continues to spread further. Attached to a noun: 「名詞＋にとどまらず」.

Pattern

noun ＋ にとどまらず

Examples

  • 被害は一地域にとどまらず、全国へと広がった。 The damage was not limited to one region; it spread nationwide.
  • この企業の影響は国内にとどまらず、海外にも広く及んでいる。 This company's influence does not stop at the domestic market but extends widely overseas as well.
