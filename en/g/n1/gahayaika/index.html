～が早いか

JLPT N1・Time / Change

Meaning

"No sooner than ~ / the instant ~." Two events occur almost simultaneously, emphasizing how quickly the second follows. Attaches to the dictionary form.

Pattern

dictionary form ＋が早いか

Examples

  • チャイムが鳴るが早いか、子供たちは教室を飛び出した。 No sooner had the bell rung than the children dashed out of the classroom.
  • 新商品が発売されるが早いか、店頭から消えた。 No sooner did the new product go on sale than it vanished from the shelves.
