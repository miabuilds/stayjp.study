～べく

JLPT N1・Cause / Reason

Meaning

"in order to~". A formal, written-style expression of purpose. More formal than 「ために」. Attaches to the dictionary form (note: 「する」 becomes 「すべく」). Often used with volitional/effortful actions taken to achieve a goal.

Pattern

dictionary form ＋べく

Examples

  • 目標を達成すべく、チーム全体で協力した。 In order to achieve the goal, the entire team worked together.
  • 一流の技術者になるべく、彼は海外留学を決意した。 In order to become a first-rate engineer, he made up his mind to study abroad.
