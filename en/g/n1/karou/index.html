～かろう

JLPT N1・Sentence-final & Written Language

Meaning

"probably ~ / likely ~". An archaic, literary equivalent of "い-adjective + だろう", attached to the い-adjective stem to express conjecture.

Pattern

i-adjective stem ＋ かろう

Examples

  • その試験は難しかろうが、準備を重ねれば突破できるはずだ。 That exam will likely be hard, but if you keep piling up preparation you should be able to get through it.
  • 真夏の屋外は暑かろうが、祭りの熱気は一見の価値がある。 The outdoors in midsummer is probably hot, but the fervor of the festival is worth seeing at least once.
