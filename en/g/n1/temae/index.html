～手前

JLPT N1・Cause / Reason

Meaning

"since I have already~ (I can't back out for the sake of appearances)". Because of one's face, position, or a prior commitment, one has no choice but to act a certain way. Attaches to the た-form or 「名詞＋の」.

Pattern

ta-form／noun の ＋手前

Examples

  • 既に約束した手前、今さら断れない。 Since I have already promised, I can't very well refuse now.
  • 後輩の手前、弱音を吐くわけにはいかない。 In front of my juniors, I can't allow myself to show weakness.
