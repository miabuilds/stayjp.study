～たが最後

JLPT N1・Others

Meaning

"once you ~, that's the end of it". Once the action is done, there is no going back / it inevitably leads to a bad outcome. Attaches to the past (た) form of a verb.

Pattern

ta-form ＋ が最後

Examples

  • あの人に秘密を打ち明けたが最後、あっという間に噂が広がる。 Once you confide a secret to that person, it's all over — the rumor spreads in no time.
  • 食べ放題に入ったが最後、食べすぎて動けなくなる。 Once you enter an all-you-can-eat place, that's it — you overeat and can't move.
