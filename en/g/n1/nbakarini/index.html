～んばかりに

JLPT N1・Sentence-ending & Written Language

Meaning

"As if about to ~" / "All but ~ing." An exaggerated expression describing someone on the verge of doing an action (or as good as saying/doing it). Formed from the ない-form minus 「ない」 plus 「んばかりに」 (「する」 becomes 「せんばかりに」).

Pattern

nai-form (drop ない) ＋ んばかりに

Examples

  • 彼女は泣かんばかりに、必死で頼み込んだ。 She pleaded desperately, as if about to burst into tears.
  • 「ぜひ試食してください」と言わんばかりに、店主が新作を差し出してきた。 The shop owner held out his new creation as if to say, "Please, do try a taste."
