～とばかりに

JLPT N1・Sentence-final & Written Language

Meaning

"as if to say ~ / with an air of ~". Attached to a quote or plain-form clause, it describes acting as though one were saying that very thing.

Pattern

plain form ＋ とばかりに

Examples

  • 「待ってました」とばかりに、彼は勢いよく手を挙げた。 As if to say "I've been waiting for this," he shot his hand up energetically.
  • 「試してごらん」とばかりに、店主が新作のパンを差し出してきた。 As if to say "Give it a try," the shopkeeper held out his newly created bread.
