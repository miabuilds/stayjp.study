～かれ～かれ

JLPT N1・Miscellaneous

Meaning

"Whether ~ or ~." Lists two opposing i-adjective stems to say that regardless of the degree, the result holds. Formed as 「い形容詞の語幹＋かれ＋（対の）い形容詞の語幹＋かれ」. Common set phrases include 「多かれ少なかれ」 (more or less) and 「遅かれ早かれ」 (sooner or later).

Pattern

i-adjective stem ＋ かれ ＋ (another) i-adjective stem ＋ かれ

Examples

  • 多かれ少なかれ、誰もがストレスを抱えている。 More or less, everyone carries some stress.
  • 遅かれ早かれ、人口減少は街の風景を変えてしまうだろう。 Sooner or later, population decline will surely change the face of the town.
