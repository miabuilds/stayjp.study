～始末だ

JLPT N1・Topic & Evaluation

Meaning

"end up in the (bad) situation of ~". After a chain of unfortunate events, things end up even worse than before. Carries a negative, exasperated tone. Attaches to the dictionary/plain form of a verb.

Pattern

plain form (dictionary form) ＋ 始末だ

Examples

  • 遅刻した上に忘れ物をし、最後には上司に叱られる始末だ。 Not only was I late, I also forgot something, and in the end I ended up getting scolded by my boss.
  • 食べすぎて、帰りの電車で気分が悪くなる始末だ。 I ate too much and ended up feeling sick on the train home.
