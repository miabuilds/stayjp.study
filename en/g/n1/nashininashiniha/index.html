～なしに・なしには

JLPT N1・Others

Meaning

"without ~ / without doing ~". Used with a noun to mean an action is (or must not be) done without the thing named. In the 「なしには～ない」 form it means "without ~, it is impossible / it won't work".

Pattern

noun ＋ なしに／なしには～ない

Examples

  • 断りなしに、人の物を使ってはいけない。 You must not use other people's things without permission.
  • 予約なしには、人気ラーメン店には入れない。 Without a reservation, you can't get into that popular ramen shop.
