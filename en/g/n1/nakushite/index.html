～なくして（は）

JLPT N1・Sentence-final & Written Language

Meaning

"Without ~, ... is impossible". Used with a following negative to emphasize that something is indispensable; without it, the outcome cannot be achieved. Formal, written language.

Pattern

noun ＋ なくして（は）～ない

Examples

  • 日々の努力なくして成功はあり得ない。 Without daily effort, success is impossible.
  • 住民の協力なくして、この地域の再生はあり得なかった。 Without the residents' cooperation, the revival of this area would have been impossible.
