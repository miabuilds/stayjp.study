～とは言い条

JLPT N1・Sentence-ending & Written Language

Meaning

"Although it is said to be ~, still..." An archaic-sounding concessive expression. More literary/formal than 「とはいえ」. Formed as 「普通形＋とは言い条（いいじょう）」.

Pattern

plain form ＋ とは言い条（いいじょう）

Examples

  • プロとは言い条、ミスを犯すこともある。 Although one is a professional, one can still make mistakes.
  • 食の都とは言い条、どの店も美味しいとは限らない。 Although it's called a culinary capital, that doesn't mean every single shop is delicious.
