～なり

JLPT N1・Time / Change

Meaning

"As soon as / the moment ~." Right after the first action is completed, an often unexpected event follows immediately. Attaches to the dictionary form.

Pattern

dictionary form ＋なり

Examples

  • 玄関に着くなり、彼はソファに倒れ込んだ。 The moment he reached the entryway, he collapsed onto the sofa.
  • 改札を出るなり、見知らぬ方に道を尋ねられた。 The moment I stepped out of the ticket gate, a stranger asked me for directions.
