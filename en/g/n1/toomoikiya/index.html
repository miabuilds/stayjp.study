～と思いきや

JLPT N1・Concession / Contrast

Meaning

"I thought~, but to my surprise…". The actual result turns out to be completely contrary to what was expected. Attaches to the plain form.

Pattern

plain form ＋と思いきや

Examples

  • 合格したと思いきや、番号を見間違えていた。 I thought I had passed, but it turned out I had misread the number.
  • 簡単なプロジェクトだと思いきや、実は非常に複雑だった。 I thought it was a simple project, but it was actually extremely complicated.
