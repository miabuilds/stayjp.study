～ともなく

JLPT N1・Topic & Evaluation

Meaning

"From somewhere ~ / without really ~ing". With a question word it expresses an uncertain, unidentifiable source; with a dictionary-form verb it expresses doing an action absent-mindedly, without conscious intent.

Pattern

question word ＋ ともなく／dictionary form ＋ ともなく

Examples

  • どこともなく甘い香りが漂ってきた。 A sweet fragrance came drifting from somewhere.
  • 見るともなくテレビを眺めていたら、見覚えのある街が映っていた。 As I was gazing at the TV without really watching, a town I recognized appeared on screen.
