～ないまでも

JLPT N1・Limitation / Emphasis

Meaning

"Even if not ~, at least…." Steps back to state a minimum expectation when the fuller goal can't be met. Attaches to the nai-form.

Pattern

nai-form ＋までも

Examples

  • 満点は取れないまでも、合格点は取りたい。 Even if I can't get a perfect score, I at least want a passing grade.
  • ネイティブのようには話せないまでも、日常会話はこなしたい。 Even if I can't speak like a native, I at least want to manage everyday conversation.
