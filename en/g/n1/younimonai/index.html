～ようにも～ない

JLPT N1・Concession & Contrast

Meaning

"Even if one wants to ~, one cannot ~." One wishes to do something but is objectively unable to. Formed as 「意向形＋にも＋可能動詞のない形」 (volitional form + にも + negative potential form of the same verb).

Pattern

volitional form ＋ にも ＋ potential verb nai-form

Examples

  • 逃げようにも逃げられない状況だった。 It was a situation where I couldn't escape even if I wanted to.
  • 予約しようにも、席が数ヶ月先まで埋まっていて予約できない。 Even if I wanted to make a reservation, I can't, because the seats are booked solid for months ahead.
