～にひきかえ

JLPT N1・Degree / Comparison

Meaning

"in contrast to~ (with a stark difference)". Emphasizes a great contrast between two things. Attaches to a noun (「名詞＋にひきかえ」). Often carries an evaluative, comparative tone.

Pattern

noun ＋にひきかえ

Examples

  • 兄の几帳面さにひきかえ、弟はいつも部屋が散らかっている。 In contrast to how meticulous my older brother is, my younger brother's room is always a mess.
  • 都会の静まり返った夜にひきかえ、下町の夜は人の声が絶えない。 In contrast to the utterly silent city nights, the nights in the old downtown never lack the sound of people's voices.
