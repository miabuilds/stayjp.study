～そばから

JLPT N1・Time / Change

Meaning

"No sooner ~ than (it happens again)." Right after doing something, it reverts to its previous state; carries a sense of frustration or futility, and typically describes a repeated situation. Attaches to the dictionary form or ta-form.

Pattern

dictionary form／ta-form ＋そばから

Examples

  • 片付けるそばから子供に散らかされる。 No sooner do I tidy up than the kids mess it up again.
  • 教わるそばから忘れてしまう。 No sooner am I taught something than I forget it.
  • 看板を拭くそばから、また砂埃がついてしまう。 No sooner do I wipe the sign than dust settles on it again.
