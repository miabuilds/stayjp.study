～とも～とも

JLPT N1・Topic & Evaluation

Meaning

"Can't tell whether it's ~ or ~". Using "noun + とも + noun + ともつかない", it expresses an ambiguous, indeterminate state where two possibilities cannot be distinguished.

Pattern

noun ＋ とも ＋ noun ＋ ともつかない

Examples

  • 笑っているのとも泣いているのともつかない表情だった。 It was an expression you couldn't tell was laughing or crying.
  • 本気とも冗談ともつかない彼の口ぶりに、最初は戸惑った。 At first I was thrown off by his tone, which I couldn't tell was serious or joking.
