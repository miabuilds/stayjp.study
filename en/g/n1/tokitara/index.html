～ときたら

JLPT N1・Topic & Evaluation

Meaning

"When it comes to ~ / As for ~". Raises a noun as a topic and then delivers a negative, usually critical or disappointed, evaluation of it.

Pattern

noun ＋ときたら

Examples

  • 最近の若手ときたら、挨拶もろくにできない。 When it comes to young people these days, they can't even greet you properly.
  • うちの上司ときたら、出張のたびにお土産の約束を忘れる。 As for my boss, every time he goes on a business trip he forgets his promise to bring back a souvenir.
