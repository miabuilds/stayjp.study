～ところを

JLPT N1・Concession / Contrast

Meaning

"at a time when (you are/it is)~". Intrudes upon a state that ought to be continuing; often used when apologizing or expressing gratitude for imposing on someone. Attaches to the plain form (often with い/な-adjectives or nouns).

Pattern

plain form ＋ところを

Examples

  • お忙しいところをお越しいただき、誠にありがとうございます。 Thank you so very much for coming all this way when you are so busy.
  • お休み中のところを恐れ入りますが、確認のお電話を差し上げました。 I'm terribly sorry to bother you during your break, but I'm calling to confirm something.
