～とあって

JLPT N1・Cause / Reason

Meaning

"because it is (a special situation)~, therefore…". Explains that a particular, notable circumstance leads to a certain result. Attaches to the plain form.

Pattern

plain form ＋とあって

Examples

  • 連休初日とあって、空港は大混雑だった。 As it was the first day of the holidays, the airport was extremely crowded.
  • 花火大会とあって、会場周辺は規制でいっぱいだった。 As it was the day of the fireworks festival, the area around the venue was full of traffic restrictions.
