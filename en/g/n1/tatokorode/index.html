～たところで

JLPT N1・Concession / Contrast

Meaning

"even if I do~, it's no use". Indicates that even doing the action will not lead to a good result. Attaches to the た-form. Often paired with 「どんなに」「いくら」 and followed by a negative or futile outcome.

Pattern

ta-form ＋ところで

Examples

  • 今さら謝ったところで、関係は戻らないだろう。 Even if you apologize now, the relationship probably won't go back to how it was.
  • どんなに急いだところで、最終便には間に合わない。 No matter how much you hurry, you won't make the last flight.
  • 慌てたところで、結果が変わるわけではない。 Panicking isn't going to change the result.
