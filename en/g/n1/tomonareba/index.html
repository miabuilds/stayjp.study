～ともなれば

JLPT N1・Topic & Evaluation

Meaning

"If/once it becomes ~". Similar to "ともなると", but with a stronger hypothetical, conditional nuance: given a certain status or occasion, such-and-such naturally follows.

Pattern

noun ＋ ともなれば

Examples

  • 代表取締役ともなれば、私生活にも世間の目が注がれる。 Once you become CEO, even your private life falls under the public eye.
  • 地域の花火大会ともなれば、街全体が一気にお祭りムードになる。 Once it's the local fireworks festival, the whole town instantly takes on a festive mood.
