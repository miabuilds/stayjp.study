～めく

JLPT N1・Miscellaneous

Meaning

"To take on the feel of ~" / "To become somewhat like ~." Indicates that something is giving off or coming to have a certain atmosphere or quality. Attached to a noun: 「名詞＋めく」 (conjugates as a verb, e.g. 「春めく」).

Pattern

noun ＋ めく

Examples

  • 一気に春めいてきた。 All at once it has started to feel like spring.
  • 提灯が並べられ、街が祭りめいてきた。 With the lanterns lined up, the streets have taken on a festival-like atmosphere.
