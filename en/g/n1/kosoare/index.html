～こそあれ

JLPT N1・Limitation / Emphasis

Meaning

"There may be ~, but certainly not…." Concedes that the first thing exists while strongly negating the latter. A written, literary expression. Attaches to a noun.

Pattern

noun ＋こそあれ

Examples

  • 彼の行動には批判こそあれ、賞賛の声はほとんど聞かれない。 His actions may draw criticism, but almost no voices of praise are heard.
  • 地元のお祭りには、地味さこそあれ、廃れる兆しはない。 The local festival may be plain, but there's no sign of it dying out.
