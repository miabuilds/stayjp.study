～や否や

JLPT N1・Time / Change

Meaning

"As soon as ~." The two actions happen almost at the same time. A written expression. Attaches to the dictionary form.

Pattern

dictionary form ＋や否や

Examples

  • 目を覚ますや否や、彼はスマホを手に取った。 The instant he woke up, he grabbed his smartphone.
  • 最終電車のアナウンスが流れるや否や、乗客が一斉にホームへ急いだ。 The moment the announcement for the last train sounded, the passengers all rushed to the platform.
