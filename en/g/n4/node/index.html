Particle「ので」(cause)

JLPT N4・Conjunctions & Cause

Meaning

Means "because ~." More polite, softer, and more objective than 「から」. Attaches to the plain form; for な-adjectives and nouns, use 「な＋ので」.

Pattern

plain form ＋ので (na-adjective/noun: な＋ので)

Examples

  • 腰が痛いので、早退します。 My back hurts, so I'm going to leave early.
  • 祝日なので、郵便局は休みです。 It's a national holiday, so the post office is closed.
