Potential form

JLPT N4・Verb Conjugation

Meaning

The potential form, meaning "can / be able to do ~." The object of the ability is usually marked with が. Godan: change the final -u sound to the -e sound and add る (書く→書ける). Ichidan: drop る and add られる. In casual speech, the ら of ichidan verbs is often dropped (食べられる→食べれる).

Pattern

godan: u-row→e-row＋る／ichidan: drop る＋られる

Examples

  • 韓国語が書けます。 I can write Korean.
  • 生卵が食べられます。 I can eat raw egg.
