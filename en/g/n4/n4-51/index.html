Volitional form

JLPT N4・Verb Conjugation

Meaning

The volitional form, meaning "let's ~" or "I think I'll ~." It is the plain-style form of suggestion/invitation. Godan (五段): change the final -u sound to the matching -ou sound (行く→行こう). Ichidan (一段): drop る and add よう. Irregulars: する→しよう, 来る→来よう.

Pattern

godan: u-row→o-row／ichidan: drop る＋よう

Examples

  • 新しい店を試そう。 Let's try out the new shop.
  • 皆で乾杯しよう。 Let's all make a toast together.
