Causative-passive form

JLPT N4・Verb Conjugation

Meaning

The causative-passive form, meaning "be made to do ~ (against one's will)." Expresses being forced or compelled to do something reluctantly. Godan: shift to the -a sound and add される (発表する uses する→させられる; 書く→書かされる). Ichidan: drop る and add させられる.

Pattern

godan: a-row＋される／ichidan: drop る＋させられる

Examples

  • 毎週会議で発表させられます。 I'm made to give a presentation at the meeting every week.
  • 子供の頃、苦手なピーマンを食べさせられました。 As a child, I was forced to eat the green peppers I disliked.
