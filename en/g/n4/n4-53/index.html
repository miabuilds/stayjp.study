Imperative form・Prohibitive form

JLPT N4・Verb Conjugation

Meaning

Imperative form = a direct order ("Do ~!"). Prohibitive form = "Don't ~! / You must not ~!" Both are very forceful and blunt. Imperative: godan verbs shift to the -e sound (走る→走れ); prohibitive is the dictionary form + な (入る→入るな).

Pattern

imperative: godan e-row／prohibitive: dictionary form＋な

Examples

  • 早く走れ！ Run faster!
  • この部屋に入るな！ Don't come into this room!
