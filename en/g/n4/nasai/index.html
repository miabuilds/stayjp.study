～なさい (command/instruction)

JLPT N4・Expression / Sentence Pattern

Meaning

A gentle command. Used by someone in a higher position toward someone lower — typically parents to children or teachers to students. Formed from the ます-stem plus なさい.

Pattern

masu-form (drop ます) ＋ なさい

Examples

  • 宿題を先にやりなさい。 Do your homework first.
  • 手をちゃんと洗いなさい。 Wash your hands properly.
