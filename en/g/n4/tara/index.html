～たら (conditional)

JLPT N4・Conditional Expressions

Meaning

Means "if / when ~ (has happened)." The most versatile and general conditional. Formed with the た-form + ら. It can also express an unexpected discovery — doing A and then finding out B.

Pattern

た form ＋ ら

Examples

  • 電車が来たら、すぐ乗ってください。 When the train comes, please get on right away.
  • ドアを開けたら、手紙が落ちていました。 When I opened the door, I found a letter had fallen on the floor.
