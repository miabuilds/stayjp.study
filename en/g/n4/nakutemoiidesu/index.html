～なくてもいいです (not necessary)

JLPT N4・Obligation & Permission

Meaning

Means "don't have to / it's okay not to do ~," expressing that an action is unnecessary. Take the ない-form, drop 「ない」, and add 「なくてもいいです」.

Pattern

nai-form (drop ない) ＋なくてもいいです

Examples

  • 今日は残業しなくてもいいです。 You don't have to work overtime today.
  • 慌てなくてもいいです。 There's no need to panic.
