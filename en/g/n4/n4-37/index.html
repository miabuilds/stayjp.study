～より…ほうが

JLPT N4・Comparison

Meaning

"Compared to B, A is more ~." Used with the pattern 「BよりAのほうが」 + adjective to say that A ranks higher than B in some quality.

Pattern

B より A のほうが ＋ adjective

Examples

  • 普通列車より特急のほうが快適です。 The express train is more comfortable than the local train.
  • 都会より田舎のほうが落ち着きます。 The countryside is more relaxing than the city.
