～たばかりです

JLPT N4・Aspect

Meaning

"Have just done ~." The 「た」-form + 「ばかりです」 emphasizes that only a short time has passed since the action was completed (this can be a subjective feeling, unlike 「たところ」 which is strictly objective).

Pattern

た form ＋ ばかりです

Examples

  • この会社に入ったばかりです。 I've just joined this company.
  • さっき歯を磨いたばかりです。 I just brushed my teeth a moment ago.
