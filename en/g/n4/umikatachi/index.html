Passive form

JLPT N4・Verb Conjugation

Meaning

The passive form, meaning "be ~ed / have ~ done to one." Covers the direct passive as well as the indirect ("adversative") passive, where the subject is affected — often negatively — by an action. Godan: shift to the -a sound and add れる (認める→認められる via the ichidan rule; 書く→書かれる). Ichidan: drop る and add られる.

Pattern

godan: a-row＋れる／ichidan: drop る＋られる

Examples

  • 課長に提案を認められました。 My proposal was approved by the section chief.
  • 駅で財布をすられました。 I had my wallet pickpocketed at the station.
