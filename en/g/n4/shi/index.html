Particle「し」(listing reasons)

JLPT N4・Conjunctions & Cause

Meaning

Lists multiple reasons or qualities: "both A and B (and so...)." Often implies there are further reasons beyond those stated. Attaches to the plain form.

Pattern

plain form ＋し

Examples

  • 眺めもいいし、サービスも丁寧だし、この旅館が好きです。 The view is great and the service is attentive, so I love this inn.
  • 雨も降っているし、疲れているし、タクシーで帰りましょう。 It's raining and I'm tired, so let's go home by taxi.
