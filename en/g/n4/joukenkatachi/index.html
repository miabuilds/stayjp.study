Conditional form (ba-form)

JLPT N4・Verb Conjugation

Meaning

The conditional ば-form, meaning "if ~ (then)." Expresses a hypothetical condition. Godan: change the final -u sound to the -e sound and add ば (合う→合えば). Ichidan: drop る and add れば.

Pattern

godan: u-row→e-row＋ば／ichidan: drop る＋れば

Examples

  • 予定が合えば、お茶でも飲みましょう。 If our schedules match, let's go for tea or something.
  • 安ければ買います。 If it's cheap, I'll buy it.
