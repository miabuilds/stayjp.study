～ばかり (doing nothing but)

JLPT N4・Expression / Sentence Pattern

Meaning

Means "be always doing (nothing but) ~." Formed with the て-form + ばかりいる. Carries a critical, disapproving tone that the person does one thing to excess.

Pattern

verb て form ＋ ばかりいる

Examples

  • 漫画ばかり読んでいないで、勉強しなさい。 Don't just read comics all the time — go study.
  • 外食ばかりしています。 I'm always eating out.
