～つもりです (intention)

JLPT N4・Expressions & Sentence Patterns

Meaning

Expresses a planned intention or decision. Use the dictionary form for what you intend to do, and the ない-form for what you intend not to do.

Pattern

dictionary form／nai-form ＋つもりです

Examples

  • 夏休みに北海道を一周するつもりです。 I'm planning to travel around Hokkaido during summer vacation.
  • 今後は甘い物を控えるつもりです。 From now on, I intend to cut back on sweets.
