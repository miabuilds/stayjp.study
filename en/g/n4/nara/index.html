Particle「なら」(conditional)

JLPT N4・Conditional Expressions

Meaning

Means "if it's the case that ~ / if you're talking about ~." It picks up a topic just raised (often by the other person) and gives advice or a suggestion based on it. Attach to the plain form.

Pattern

plain form ＋ なら

Examples

  • 温泉に行くなら、箱根がおすすめですよ。 If you're going to a hot spring, I recommend Hakone.
  • 家賃が予算内なら、契約したいです。 If the rent is within my budget, I'd like to sign the contract.
