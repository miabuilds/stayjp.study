～ても (concessive condition)

JLPT N4・Conjunctions & Cause

Meaning

Means "even if ~ / even though ~," expressing that the result holds despite the condition. Attach 「も」 to the て-form. Often paired with 「何度～ても」("no matter how many times ~").

Pattern

te-form ＋も

Examples

  • 体調が悪くても、試合に出ます。 Even if I don't feel well, I'll play in the match.
  • 何度読んでも、意味がわかりません。 No matter how many times I read it, I can't understand the meaning.
