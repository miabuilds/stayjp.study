Honorific form (れる・られる)

JLPT N4・Verb Conjugation

Meaning

The passive form (れる・られる) can also be used to show respect toward the subject (honorific/尊敬語). The form is identical to the passive: for godan verbs use the あ-row + れる; for ichidan verbs drop る + られる. Context tells you whether it's passive or honorific.

Pattern

godan: a-row＋れる／ichidan: drop る＋られる

Examples

  • 社長はもう出発されました。 The company president has already departed. (honorific)
  • 課長は何を注文されますか。 What will the section chief order? (honorific)
