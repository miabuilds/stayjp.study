～てきます・～ていきます

JLPT N4・Aspect

Meaning

The 「て」-form + 「きます」 indicates movement toward the speaker or a change developing up to the present; the 「て」-form + 「いきます」 indicates movement away from the speaker or a change continuing on into the future.

Pattern

te-form ＋きます (toward speaker)／te-form ＋いきます (away from speaker)

Examples

  • 街の雰囲気が変わってきました。 The atmosphere of the town has gradually changed (up to now).
  • これから物価はもっと上がっていきます。 From now on prices will keep going up.
