Particle「でも」(universal affirmation)

JLPT N4・Colloquial / Particles

Meaning

Means "no matter ~ / any ~ at all." A question word + "でも" expresses total, unrestricted affirmation, covering every possible case (anywhere, anytime, anyone, anything, etc.).

Pattern

question word ＋ でも

Examples

  • どこでも勉強できます。 I can study anywhere.
  • いつでも連絡してください。 Please contact me anytime.
