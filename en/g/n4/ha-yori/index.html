～は...より (comparison)

JLPT N4・Comparison

Meaning

Means "A is more ... than B." A is the topic being described, and B is the standard it is compared against.

Pattern

A は B より＋adjective

Examples

  • 名古屋は札幌より気温が高いです。 Nagoya has a higher temperature than Sapporo.
  • 地下鉄はバスより時間が正確です。 The subway is more punctual than the bus.
