Keigo overview (お/ご・ございます)

JLPT N4・Honorific Language (Keigo)

Meaning

An overview of polite/beautifying prefixes and words. お attaches to native Japanese (和語) words and ご to Sino-Japanese (漢語) words to make them polite (お名前, ご住所). ございます is the polite equivalent of あります ("there is / to have").

Pattern

お＋native Japanese word／ご＋Sino-Japanese word (beautifying language)

Examples

  • ご予約は何時ですか。 What time is your reservation?
  • 何かございますか。 Is there something I can help you with? / Is anything the matter?
