～がります (third-person desire)

JLPT N4・Quotation / Thought

Meaning

Describes a third person's desire or feeling from an outside perspective. Attach 「がる／がっている」 to the stem of a 「たい」 form or a desire adjective (drop the final 「い」), since you cannot state another person's feelings as direct fact.

Pattern

i-adjective (drop い) ＋ がります

Examples

  • 弟が新しいゲーム機を欲しがっています。 My younger brother wants a new game console.
  • 姪は海外で働きたがっています。 My niece wants to work abroad.
