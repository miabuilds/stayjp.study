Formal noun「こと」

JLPT N4・Expressions / Sentence Patterns

Meaning

The formal noun "こと" nominalizes a verb phrase, turning a whole clause into a noun. Similar to "の," but more written/abstract and preferred for stating general facts, rules, or ideas.

Pattern

plain form ＋ こと

Examples

  • 私の生きがいは花を育てることです。 My joy in life is growing flowers.
  • 時間を守ることは信頼の基本です。 Keeping to your schedule is the basis of trust.
