～そうです (appearance/inference)

JLPT N4・Conjecture / Appearance

Meaning

"It looks like ~ / seems about to ~." A guess based on visual appearance. Attach 「そう」 to the 「ます」-stem of verbs (drop 「ます」), to い-adjectives (drop 「い」), or directly to な-adjectives.

Pattern

masu-form (drop ます) ＋ そうです／i-adjective (drop い) ＋ そう／na-adjective ＋ そう

Examples

  • 台風が来そうです。 It looks like a typhoon is coming.
  • あのスープは熱そうです。 That soup looks hot.
