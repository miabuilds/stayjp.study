～ついでに

JLPT N3・Time

Meaning

"While one is at it / on the same occasion." Indicates doing a second thing incidentally while doing a main action. Attaches to dictionary form, ~た form, or 「名詞＋の」.

Pattern

dictionary form／ta-form ＋ ついでに／noun の ＋ ついでに

Examples

  • 買い物のついでに、クリーニングを取りに行った。 While I was out shopping, I went to pick up the dry cleaning.
  • 散歩するついでに、手紙を投函した。 While taking a walk, I dropped a letter in the mailbox.
  • 本屋に寄ったついでに、文房具も買ってきた。 Since I had stopped by the bookstore anyway, I also bought some stationery.
