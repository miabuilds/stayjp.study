～ても

JLPT N3・Condition & Hypothesis

Meaning

"Even if ~" / "even though ~." Expresses a concessive condition: the result holds regardless of the preceding circumstance. Attaches to the て-form (「でも」 after nouns/な-adjectives). Often paired with question words (どんなに, 何度) for "no matter how ~."

Pattern

te-form ＋ も／でも

Examples

  • 台風が来ても、予定通り出発する。 Even if a typhoon comes, we'll depart as scheduled.
  • 何度確認しても、ミスが見つからない。 No matter how many times I check, I can't find the mistake.
  • どんなに忙しくても、朝ご飯は欠かさない。 No matter how busy I am, I never skip breakfast.
