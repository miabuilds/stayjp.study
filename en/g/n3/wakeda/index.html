～わけだ

JLPT N3・Assertion / Emphasis

Meaning

"That is to say ~ / no wonder ~." It presents a conclusion that follows naturally and logically from the given circumstances.

Pattern

plain form ＋ わけだ

Examples

  • 毎朝走っているから、マラソンが得意なわけだ。 He runs every morning, so no wonder he's good at marathons.
  • ずっと海外勤務だったから、英語が流暢なわけだ。 He worked abroad the whole time, so no wonder his English is fluent.
  • コーヒーを3杯飲んだから、夜眠れないわけだ。 I drank three cups of coffee, so no wonder I can't sleep at night.
