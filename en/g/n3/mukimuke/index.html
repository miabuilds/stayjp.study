～向き・～向け

JLPT N3・Manner / Tendency

Meaning

「向き」 = "suited to / suitable for ~" (naturally a good fit for a certain group). 「向け」 = "designed for / aimed at ~" (intentionally targeted at a certain group).

Pattern

noun ＋ 向き／noun ＋ 向け

Examples

  • この味付けは子供向きだ。 This seasoning is well suited to children.
  • 初心者向けのプログラミング講座。 A programming course for beginners.
  • この教材は社会人向けに作られている。 This teaching material is designed for working adults.
