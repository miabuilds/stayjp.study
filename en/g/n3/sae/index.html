～さえ

JLPT N3・Others

Meaning

"Even ~" / "not even ~." Emphasizes an extreme example, implying that if even this (unexpected) case holds, other cases certainly do too. Attaches to a noun (which may take a particle, e.g. 「にさえ」).

Pattern

noun ＋ さえ

Examples

  • 多忙すぎて、食事する時間さえない。 I'm so busy I don't even have time to eat.
  • 漢字はもちろん、ひらがなさえまだ読めない。 Not to mention kanji, I can't even read hiragana yet.
  • 家族にさえ、まだ転職を話していない。 I haven't even told my family that I'm changing jobs yet.
