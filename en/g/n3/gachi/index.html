～がち

JLPT N3・Manner / Tendency

Meaning

"Tends to ~ / is apt to ~." Expresses a frequent or habitual tendency, most often used for negative or undesirable ones. Attaches to the verb ます-stem + がち, or 「名詞＋がち」.

Pattern

verb masu-form ＋ がち／noun ＋ がち

Examples

  • 冬は風邪を引きがちだ。 In winter I tend to catch colds.
  • 一人暮らしは栄養が偏りがちだ。 Living alone, one's diet tends to become unbalanced.
  • 忙しいと、食事を抜きがちになる。 When I'm busy, I tend to skip meals.
