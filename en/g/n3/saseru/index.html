～させる（使役）

JLPT N3・Others

Meaning

Causative: "make/let someone do ~." Expresses either forcing someone to do something or permitting them to do it. Formation: Group-1 (五段) verbs change the final syllable to the あ-row + せる; Group-2 (一段) verbs take させる; 「する」→「させる」, 「くる」→「こさせる」.

Pattern

verb causative form: godan → a-sound stem ＋ せる, ichidan → させる

Examples

  • 母は子供に宿題を終わらせた。 The mother made her child finish the homework.
  • 店長はスタッフに閉店作業を任せた。 The store manager had the staff take charge of the closing work.
  • リーダーはメンバーに役割を分担させた。 The leader had the members divide up the roles.
