～わけにはいかない

JLPT N3・Assertion / Emphasis

Meaning

"Can't (bring myself to) ~" (with the dictionary form) or "can't not ~ / have no choice but to ~" (with the ない form). It expresses being unable to do (or unable to avoid) something because of social obligation, morals, or one's position.

Pattern

dictionary form／nai-form ＋ わけにはいかない

Examples

  • 明日が納期だから、今日は帰るわけにはいかない。 The deadline is tomorrow, so I can't just go home today.
  • 招待してくれた以上、断るわけにはいかない。 Since they invited me, I can't very well turn it down.
  • 上司の送別会を欠席するわけにはいかない。 I can't skip my boss's farewell party.
