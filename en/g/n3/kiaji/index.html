～気味

JLPT N3・Manner / Tendency

Meaning

"A bit ~ / slightly ~." Indicates a mild sign or slight tendency toward some state, usually a negative one. Attaches to the verb ます-stem + 気味, or 「名詞＋気味」.

Pattern

verb masu-form ＋ 気味／noun ＋ 気味

Examples

  • 最近、太り気味だ。 Lately I've been putting on a bit of weight.
  • 風邪気味なので、今日は早く寝ます。 I feel like I'm coming down with a cold, so I'll go to bed early today.
  • 疲れ気味だから、今週末はゆっくり休もう。 I'm feeling a bit tired, so let's take it easy and rest this weekend.
