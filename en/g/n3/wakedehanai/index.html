～わけではない

JLPT N3・Assertion / Emphasis

Meaning

"It's not that ~ / it doesn't mean that ~." A partial denial used to clear up a misunderstanding, correcting an assumption the listener might have drawn.

Pattern

plain form ＋ わけではない

Examples

  • 嫌いなわけではないが、進んで選ばない。 It's not that I dislike it, I just wouldn't actively choose it.
  • 反対しているわけではありません。 It's not that I'm opposed to it.
  • 忙しいわけではないが、気が重くて動けない。 It's not that I'm busy; I just feel down and can't get moving.
