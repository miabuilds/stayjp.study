～間に

JLPT N3・Time

Meaning

"During ~ / while ~." Indicates that something happens at some point within a certain period of time. The action in the main clause typically occurs once, at an unspecified moment, before that period ends. Attaches to dictionary form, the ~ている form, or 「名詞＋の」.

Pattern

dictionary form ＋ 間に／ている ＋ 間に／noun の ＋ 間に

Examples

  • 留守の間に宅配便が届いた。 A parcel was delivered while I was out.
  • シャワーを浴びている間に、電話が鳴っていた。 The phone was ringing while I was taking a shower.
  • 出張で不在の間に、会社で人事異動が発表された。 While I was away on a business trip, a personnel reshuffle was announced at the company.
