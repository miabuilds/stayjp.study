～おかげで

JLPT N3・Connectives / Cause

Meaning

Means "thanks to ~." States a positive cause and carries a tone of gratitude. Use plain form + "おかげで" or "noun + の + おかげで."

Pattern

plain form ＋ おかげで／noun の ＋ おかげで

Examples

  • 先輩のおかげで、無事に納品できました。 Thanks to my senior colleague, I was able to deliver the goods without a hitch.
  • 天気がよかったおかげで、ハイキングが楽しめた。 Thanks to the good weather, we could enjoy the hike.
  • 奨学金のおかげで、海外留学が実現した。 Thanks to the scholarship, studying abroad became a reality.
