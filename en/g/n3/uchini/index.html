～うちに

JLPT N3・Time

Meaning

Means "while ~ / during the time that ~ (before it changes)." You do something taking advantage of a current state, implying that the opportunity or condition won't last. Connects to dictionary/ない-form, i-adjective, な-adjective + な, or "noun + の."

Pattern

dictionary form／nai-form／i-adjective い／na-adjective な／noun の ＋ うちに

Examples

  • 若いうちにいろんな国を旅しておきたい。 I want to travel to many countries while I'm still young.
  • 記憶が新しいうちに、議事録をまとめよう。 Let's write up the minutes while the memory is still fresh.
  • 明るいうちに、山から下山しましょう。 Let's come down from the mountain while it's still light out.
