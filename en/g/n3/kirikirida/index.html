～きり・～きりだ

JLPT N3・Scope / Limitation

Meaning

"Just / only ~," or "ever since ~, (nothing further has happened)." Expresses either a limitation to a single scope, or that after one occurrence a state has continued unchanged. Attaches to the ~た form + きり, or 「名詞＋きり」.

Pattern

ta-form ＋ きり／noun ＋ きり

Examples

  • 二人きりでじっくり話したい。 I want to talk with you at length, just the two of us.
  • 同窓会以来、会ったきりで連絡が途絶えている。 I met them at the class reunion, but since then we've been out of touch.
  • その店には一度行ったきり、また行く機会がない。 I went to that shop just once and haven't had the chance to go again.
