～をはじめ（として）

JLPT N3・Scope / Limitation

Meaning

"Starting with / such as ~ (among others)." Cites one representative example to imply that others of the same kind also apply. Attaches to 「名詞＋をはじめ（として）」.

Pattern

noun ＋ をはじめ（として）

Examples

  • 京都をはじめ、奈良や金沢にも行きたい。 Starting with Kyoto, I also want to visit Nara and Kanazawa.
  • 役員をはじめとして、全社員がチャリティーに協力した。 Starting with the executives, all the employees cooperated in the charity event.
  • ラーメンをはじめ、寿司や天ぷらも外国人に人気だ。 Ramen, as well as sushi and tempura, are popular with foreigners.
