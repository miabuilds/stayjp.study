～てくる・～ていく

JLPT N3・Others

Meaning

"~ come to / begin to" and "~ go on / continue to." Expresses the direction of an action (toward or away from the speaker) or a trend of change over time: 「てくる」 for change up to the present, 「ていく」 for change continuing into the future. Attaches to the て-form.

Pattern

te-form ＋ くる／te-form ＋ いく

Examples

  • 秋になり、朝晩が冷えこんできた。 Autumn has come, and the mornings and evenings have grown cold. (change continuing up to now)
  • これからも地道に努力していくつもりだ。 I intend to keep making steady efforts from now on.
  • ここ数年、オンライン会議が増えてきた。 Over the past few years, online meetings have been increasing.
