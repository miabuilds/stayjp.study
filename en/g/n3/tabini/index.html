～たびに

JLPT N3・Others

Meaning

"Every time ~ / whenever ~." It indicates that each time an action or event occurs, something else regularly happens along with it.

Pattern

dictionary form ＋ たびに／noun ＋ の ＋ たびに

Examples

  • この写真を見るたびに、学生時代を思い出す。 Every time I look at this photo, I remember my student days.
  • 出張のたびにお土産を買って帰る。 Every time I go on a business trip, I buy souvenirs to bring home.
  • 季節が変わるたびに、体調を崩しやすい。 Every time the seasons change, I tend to fall ill.
