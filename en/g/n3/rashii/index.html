～らしい

JLPT N3・Others

Meaning

Two uses: (1) "apparently / I hear that ~" (an inference based on hearsay or information from an outside source); (2) "typical of / just like ~" (attached to a noun to say something is very characteristic of it).

Pattern

plain form ＋ らしい

Examples

  • 来週から気温が下がるらしい。 Apparently the temperature will drop starting next week.
  • 彼の発言はいかにも彼らしい。 His remark is so typical of him.
  • あの会社は来月リモートワークを廃止するらしい。 Apparently that company is going to abolish remote work next month.
