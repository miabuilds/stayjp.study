～たところ

JLPT N3・Condition / Hypothesis

Meaning

"When/after I did ~, (I found that) ..." Indicates that upon doing something, a particular result or discovery followed. The outcome is often unexpected or newly learned. Attaches to the ~た form + ところ.

Pattern

ta-form ＋ ところ

Examples

  • 窓口に問い合わせたところ、すぐに回答を得た。 When I inquired at the counter, I got an answer right away.
  • 詳しく調べたところ、原因が判明した。 When I investigated in detail, the cause became clear.
  • 新しい炊飯器を試したところ、思ったより簡単だった。 When I tried out the new rice cooker, it was easier than I had expected.
