～くらい・～ぐらい (degree)

JLPT N3・Comparison / Degree

Meaning

"To the extent that ~." Like 「ほど」, it illustrates a degree by giving an example, and is often used for extreme degrees. Slightly more colloquial than 「ほど」. Attaches to dictionary form / 「名詞」 + くらい／ぐらい.

Pattern

dictionary form／noun ＋ くらい

Examples

  • 手が震えるくらい緊張した。 I was so nervous that my hands were shaking.
  • 声が出ないくらい驚いた。 I was so surprised I couldn't get a word out.
  • 朝の通勤時間は動けないぐらい電車が混む。 During the morning commute, the trains are so crowded you can't even move.
