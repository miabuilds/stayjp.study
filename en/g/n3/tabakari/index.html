～たばかり

JLPT N3・Time

Meaning

Means "just did ~ / only just ~," stressing that an action was completed a very short time ago. Must attach to the た-form: sentence-final "~たばかりだ," connective "~たばかりで," or noun-modifying "~たばかりの." Note: "dictionary form + ばかりだ" (e.g. 悪化するばかりだ) is a different grammar meaning "keeps getting more and more ~"—don't confuse the two.

Pattern

verb ta-form ＋ ばかり (だ／で／の)

Examples

  • 帰国したばかりで、まだ時差ボケが残っている。 I just got back to my home country, so I'm still jet-lagged.
  • 起きたばかりで、頭がぼんやりしている。 I just woke up, so my head is still foggy.
  • このプロジェクトに配属されたばかりで、右も左もわからない。 I've just been assigned to this project, so I don't know my way around at all.
