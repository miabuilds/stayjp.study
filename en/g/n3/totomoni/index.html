～とともに

JLPT N3・Connectives / Cause

Meaning

Means "along with / as ~ / together with ~." Expresses a change happening in parallel with another (as one thing progresses, so does another) or doing something jointly with someone. Follows a noun or a dictionary-form verb.

Pattern

noun ＋ とともに／dictionary form ＋ とともに

Examples

  • 年齢とともに体力が落ちてきた。 My physical strength has declined with age.
  • 家族とともに新居で新年を迎えた。 I welcomed the new year in our new home together with my family.
  • 技術の発展とともに、働き方も多様化してきた。 Along with the advance of technology, ways of working have also diversified.
