～っぱなし

JLPT N3・Manner / Tendency

Meaning

"Leave something in a certain state and just leave it that way." It indicates that the result of an action continues unattended, and carries a negative nuance (something was left undone or neglected).

Pattern

verb masu-form ＋ っぱなし

Examples

  • テレビをつけっぱなしで寝てしまった。 I fell asleep with the TV left on.
  • 書類がデスクに散らかしっぱなしだ。 The documents have been left scattered all over the desk.
  • 窓を開けっぱなしにして出かけてしまった。 I went out and left the window wide open.
