～反面

JLPT N3・Comparison & Degree

Meaning

"On the other hand ~" / "while (conversely) ~." Presents a contrasting or opposite side of the same thing, pairing a positive point with a negative one (or vice versa). Attaches to the plain form (な-adjectives take 「な反面」, nouns take 「である反面」).

Pattern

plain form ＋ 反面

Examples

  • 彼は仕事が速い反面、細かいミスが多い。 He is fast at his work, but on the other hand he makes a lot of small mistakes.
  • 都会は便利な反面、人間関係が希薄だ。 The city is convenient, but on the other hand human relationships are shallow.
  • このソフトは機能が豊富な反面、操作が複雑だ。 This software is rich in features, but on the other hand it is complicated to operate.
