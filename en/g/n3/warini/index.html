～わりに

JLPT N3・Comparison / Degree

Meaning

"For ~ / considering ~, (surprisingly) ..." Indicates that the actual situation differs from what one would expect given the stated standard. Attaches to the plain form + わりに, or 「名詞＋の＋わりに」.

Pattern

plain form ＋ わりに／noun の ＋ わりに

Examples

  • このレストランは値段が手頃なわりに、料理の質が高い。 For how reasonable its prices are, this restaurant's food is surprisingly high quality.
  • 年齢のわりに、肌が若々しい。 For her age, her skin is remarkably youthful.
  • 広告のわりに、実物は小さかった。 For what the advertisement suggested, the actual item was small.
