～てもらう・～てあげる・～てくれる

JLPT N3・Others

Meaning

Giving-and-receiving verbs. 「てもらう」 = "have/get someone to do (for me)." 「てあげる」 = "do (something) for someone else." 「てくれる」 = "someone does (something) for me." All attach to the て-form; the direction of the favor depends on which verb is used.

Pattern

te-form ＋ もらう／あげる／くれる

Examples

  • 同僚に書類を確認してもらった。 I had a colleague check the documents for me.
  • 父が誕生日に時計を買ってくれた。 My father bought me a watch for my birthday.
  • 妹に勉強を教えてあげた。 I taught my younger sister her schoolwork.
