### n5-42
- **ex**
  - 舊:The most important conjugation. For godan verbs (sound changes): く→いて, ぐ→いで, す→して, む/ぶ/ぬ→んで, う/つ/る→って. Exception: 行く→行って.
  - 新:The most important conjugation, used for requests (〜てください), linking/sequencing actions, and the progressive/state form (〜ている). For godan verbs (sound changes): く→いて, ぐ→いで, す→して, む/ぶ/ぬ→んで, う/つ/る→って. For ichidan verbs: drop る + て (e.g., 見る→見て). Exception: 行く→行って.

### n5-43
- **ex**
  - 舊:Dictionary form = plain-style affirmative. Nai form = plain-style negative. For godan verbs ending in -u: う→わない.
  - 新:Dictionary form = plain-style affirmative. Nai form = plain-style negative. To form the nai form: godan verbs change the final -u sound to the -a sound + ない (and う→わない, e.g., 買う→買わない); ichidan verbs drop る + ない (e.g., 起きる→起きない).

### n5-44
- **ex**

  - 新:Past tense, plain style. The た form is built exactly like the て form, replacing て with た (and で with だ): for godan verbs use the sound changes (e.g., 話す→話した, 急ぐ→急いだ); for ichidan verbs drop る + た (e.g., 見る→見た). The negative past (nakatta form) changes ない→なかった.
