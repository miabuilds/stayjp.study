How to use

30 秒上手Get started in 30 seconds

不用註冊也能先逛。想存進度就用 Google 登入,還送免費試用(網頁 3 天、iOS App 7 天)。以下是最順的學習流程:Browse without signing up. To save progress, sign in with Google — a free trial is included (3 days on web, 7 days on the iOS app). Here's the smoothest way to study:

  1. 選你的程度Pick your level

    第一次進來選 N5–N1,之後隨時可改。不確定就從 N5 開始。Choose N5–N1 on first visit (changeable anytime). Not sure? Start at N5.

  2. 背單字卡Study vocab cards

    「單字」分頁一張張看,點卡片就唸出來,每個字都有例句與翻譯。想重點複習就按 ★ 收藏。Open the Vocab tab. Tap a card to hear it — every word has an example and translation. Star ★ the ones you want to revisit.

  3. 學文法Learn grammar

    「文法」分頁看接續與語感,點例句方框就唸,幫你抓語感。In the Grammar tab, read usage notes and tap the example box to hear it to build a feel for it.

  4. 模擬考Take a mock exam

    想測實力就做模考,考完看成績、可分享成績卡。Test yourself with a mock exam, see your score, and share a result card.

  5. 每天複習(SRS)Review daily (SRS)

    每天回來清「複習」,系統依你的記憶曲線安排,不熟的自動多見幾次——這是背起來的關鍵。Come back daily to clear your Reviews. The system schedules them to your memory curve — weak items reappear more often. This is what makes it stick.

小技巧Tips

  • 通勤、排隊的零碎時間背幾張卡,累積很快。Study a few cards during commutes or waiting — it adds up fast.
  • 跟著例句「跟讀」練發音與語調。Shadow the example sentences to practice pronunciation and intonation.
  • 易混淆的近義詞,用「易混淆詞比較」一次搞懂。Sort out similar words with the confusable-words comparison.
  • 免費版每個工具每天都能用;想無限次再升級 Premium。Free gives you each tool daily; upgrade to Premium for unlimited use.
