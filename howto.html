How to use

30 秒上手Get started in 30 seconds

不用註冊也能先逛。想存進度就用 Google 登入,還送免費試用。以下是最順的學習流程:Browse without signing up. To save progress, sign in with Google — a free trial is included. Here's the smoothest way to study:

  1. 選你的程度Pick your level

    第一次進來選 N5–N1,之後隨時可改。不確定就從 N5 開始。Choose N5–N1 on first visit (changeable anytime). Not sure? Start at N5.

  2. 背單字卡Study vocab cards

    「單字」分頁一張張看,點卡片就唸出來,每個字都有例句與翻譯。想重點複習就按 ★ 收藏。Open the Vocab tab. Tap a card to hear it — every word has an example and translation. Star ★ the ones you want to revisit.

  3. 學文法Learn grammar

    「文法」分頁看接續與語感,點例句方框就唸,幫你抓語感。In the Grammar tab, read usage notes and tap the example box to hear it to build a feel for it.

  4. 模擬考Take a mock exam

    想測實力就做模考,考完看成績、可分享成績卡。Test yourself with a mock exam, see your score, and share a result card.

  5. 每天複習(SRS)Review daily (SRS)

    每天回來清「複習」,系統依你的記憶曲線安排,不熟的自動多見幾次——這是背起來的關鍵。Come back daily to clear your Reviews. The system schedules them to your memory curve — weak items reappear more often. This is what makes it stick.

不只單字卡:全部功能Beyond flashcards: everything inside

單字卡 + 朗讀Vocab cards + audio
每個字附例句、翻譯與真人般語音,點卡就唸。Every word has an example, translation, and natural audio — tap to hear.
文法 + 詳解Grammar + explanations
接續、語感與例句朗讀;測驗答錯自動附上解說卡。Usage, nuance, spoken examples; wrong answers get an explanation card.
動詞變化Verb conjugation
て形、可能、意向、使役、受身、自他動詞逐項練。Drill て-form, potential, volitional, causative, passive, transitivity.
生活會話(分類)Everyday phrases (by scene)
用餐、交通、購物、住宿、求助等場景分類,一按就唸。Food, transport, shopping, hotel, help — grouped by scene, tap to hear.
文章朗讀(可調語速)Article reading (adjustable speed)
分級短文邊聽邊讀,語速 −/+ 自己調,還有今日故事。Leveled passages read aloud; adjust speed with −/+, plus a daily story.
計時模擬考Timed mock exam
仿真題型計時作答,看分數、分享成績卡。Realistic timed questions, scored, with a shareable result card.
SRS 間隔複習SRS spaced review
依記憶曲線排程,不熟的自動多見幾次。Scheduled to your memory curve; weak items reappear more often.
字體大小可調Adjustable text size
按「A」放大全站文字,看得更舒服。Tap "A" to enlarge text across the whole app for easier reading.

小技巧Tips

  • 通勤、排隊的零碎時間背幾張卡,累積很快。Study a few cards during commutes or waiting — it adds up fast.
  • 跟著例句「跟讀」練發音與語調。Shadow the example sentences to practice pronunciation and intonation.
  • 易混淆的近義詞,用「易混淆詞比較」一次搞懂。Sort out similar words with the confusable-words comparison.
  • 讀文章覺得太快?用播放列的 −/+ 把語速調到你聽得懂的速度。Article too fast? Use the −/+ on the player to set a speed you can follow.
  • 字太小就按右上的「A」放大;整站文字(含測驗彈窗)都會跟著變大。Text too small? Tap "A" to enlarge everything, quiz pop-ups included.
  • 加到主畫面就能當 App 用,通勤沒網路也能背(PWA 離線)。Add to your home screen to use it like an app — works offline on the go (PWA).
  • 免費版每個工具每天都能用;想無限次再升級 Premium。Free gives you each tool daily; upgrade to Premium for unlimited use.
